Zillow Group operates the largest US residential property portal, monetising homebuyer traffic through agent advertising and through mortgages via Zillow Home Loans. It is not a REIT despite the housing-sector tagging: it owns no meaningful property. The 2021 iBuying exit burned roughly half a billion dollars and reset the strategy around its core marketplace and rentals. Results track US housing transaction volumes and agent marketing budgets, both of which fell sharply as mortgage rates rose, so the equity is a geared bet on transaction activity recovering.
