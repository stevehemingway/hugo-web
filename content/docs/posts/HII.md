# Huntingdon-Ingalls

This is a boat builder. OK, so not many boats have been deployed in Ukraine, but they may be in Taiwan.

I got thinking about defence contractors when I read [this](https://mattstoller.substack.com/p/the-military-industrial-stock-buyback).
Matt Stoler is bemoaning the corruption and anti-competitive aspects of the industry, so it feels wrong to 
direct some money to fund it, but Stoler does explain very clearly why the risk profile of a big defence contractor 
is very different to an ordinary sort of business.

I did some research, for a change. Not a lot but some!
I got a list of promising defence stocks [here](https://www.investopedia.com/top-defense-stocks-q2-2023-7376223), and went through them.
Most have already taken off (check out Bombardier!), but HII seems to be a laggard.

I did think about [[BA]] and [[LDO.BVME]] and [[RR]] but I decided on HII as more of a pure play.
I think it does more actual manufacturing than $RTX, and is less a financial engineering play, 
but it seems to have a pretty decent return on equity.
