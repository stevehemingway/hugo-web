# Silvergate

[**Silvergate Capital** (NYSE: SI — $868 million) describes itself as “the leading provider of innovative financial infrastructure solutions and services to participants in the nascent and expanding digital currency industry.” The company also frequently touts its Silvergate Exchange Network (SEN), a “proprietary, virtually instantaneous payment network” that has processed a total of over $1 trillion in transactions. The Bear Cave believes Silvergate’s potential regulatory infractions, shrinking deposit base, and diminishing credibility are not being fully recognized by the market.](https://thebearcave.substack.com/p/problems-at-silvergate-capital-si)
