Amplia Therapeutics (ASX: ATX, OTCQB: INNMF) is a Melbourne-based clinical-stage drug developer working on inhibitors of Focal Adhesion Kinase, a target relevant to both cancer and fibrosis. Its lead asset, narmafotinib (AMP945), is in a phase 2 trial in pancreatic cancer, with ovarian cancer and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis behind it; the FDA has granted Orphan Drug Designation in two of those indications. The pipeline originated at the Australian Cancer Therapeutics CRC. It is a pre-revenue microcap whose value rests entirely on clinical readouts, kept alive by R&D tax rebates and periodic raises, and it carries the dilution and going-concern risk that goes with that.
