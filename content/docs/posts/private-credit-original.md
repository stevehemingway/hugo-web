## Key Investment Themes

**Private Credit Bubble Risk:**
- Rising rates crushing portfolio values
- Covenant-lite loans coming home to roost
- Commercial real estate exposure concerns
- Liquidity mismatch in open-end funds

**AI Infrastructure Boom:**
- $200B+ debt raised for data centers in 2025
- Private credit stepping in where banks fear to tread
- Massive CapEx requirements for AI workloads
- Power constraints driving innovation

**Listed vs Private Markets:**
- BDCs trading at discounts to NAV
- Private funds raising record capital
- Institutional money flowing to mega-funds
- Retail investors locked out of best deals

## Risk Factors

**For Private Credit:**
- Interest rate sensitivity
- Credit quality deterioration
- Liquidity risk in open-end structures
- Regulatory scrutiny of private markets

**For AI Infrastructure:**
- Overbuilding risk in data centers
- Power grid constraints
- Technology obsolescence
- Customer concentration risk
