Twist Bioscience is a synthetic biology company that manufactures synthetic DNA by writing it on silicon chips rather than in plates or columns, which is what underpins its cost position. It sells synthetic genes, NGS target-enrichment panels, sample-preparation reagents and antibody libraries for drug discovery. The company has run at a loss throughout its listed life, and the bull case has always rested on operating leverage arriving as volumes scale rather than on any single product.
