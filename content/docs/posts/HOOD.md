Robinhood Markets runs the commission-free brokerage app that pulled a generation of retail investors into US equities, options and, more recently, crypto. Revenue is not primarily from client fees but from payment for order flow, plus net interest income on customer cash and margin, which makes the top line highly sensitive to trading volumes and to the interest-rate cycle. The bear case rests on that cyclicality and on the concentration of revenue in a handful of market-maker counterparties; the bull case on a young, growing funded-customer base and expanding product surface. Tagged here as a market thesis rather than a single-company one.
