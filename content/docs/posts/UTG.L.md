## comment 2023-5-22 (P: ~901p)

This largely is in the business of providing student housing.
I think that this has limited growth, but the endless flow of foreign students with very few options outside 
of purpose-built accommodation make keep the company going.

It is trading on 15 times rental income, and has historically provided a dividend yield of 1.4%.
It is forecast to be able to pay a 5% yield, which will, if it is achieved, support the price.
My best guess is that it will eventually fail, but the price action is not too bad. (22 May 2023).
