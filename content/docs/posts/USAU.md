# U.S. Gold Corp. ($USAU)

## Campaign

**Published:** 16 Nov 2026, 13:07
**Market cap:** US$175.7m
**Direction:** Short
**Analyst:** Shortfinder (via Black Styx Capital)

Shortfinder's follow-up short thesis rests on a stalled mine build and a permit clock:

- **Construction paused.** Work at the CK Gold project has stopped, and Wyoming's Industrial Siting Permit expires in December 2027 unless financing is secured.
- **The financing decision slipped.** A targeted late-2026 financing and construction decision has not occurred.
- **Cash is thin against the build.** Cash fell to US$27.1m at 31 July 2026, while the estimated build cost of roughly US$422m now equates to about 2.4x the company's market value.
- **Dilution is coming.** The company filed a US$150m universal shelf. Shortfinder expects a package weighted heavily toward equity, and argues the company's bargaining position weakens as the permit deadline approaches.
