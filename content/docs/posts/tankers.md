## Key Points

- **Crude tankers** (VLCC, Suezmax, Aframax) move unrefined oil from producers to refineries
- **Product tankers** (MR, LR1, LR2) move refined products (gasoline, diesel, jet fuel) from refineries to markets
- Most companies are incorporated in **Bermuda or Monaco** for tax reasons but trade on US exchanges
- Many operate on **spot markets** (volatile earnings) + **time charters** (predictable cash flow)
