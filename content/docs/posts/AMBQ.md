# Ambiq Micro (AMBQ)

<blockquote>
AMBQ, a fabless ultra-low power semiconductor solutions supplier, excites investors on the strength of “Edge AI,” meaning its products allow consumer devices like fitness trackers run AI models in the background without overheating or wearing out the battery. Yet the company continuously loses money and produces negative operating cash flows despite growing revenue and over 300 million products shipped since inception in 2010 – the company is long past its startup honeymoon phase. AMBQ appears to have limited ability to gain economies of scale, likely because of intense competition and weak pricing power; there is still no clear path to profitability. Increasing revenue while continuing with similar bottom-line losses is not an attractive investment proposition when a company has already been around for about 16 years. Management has been selling a significant number of shares since lock-up periods ended in 2026, while investors have endured two very large dilutive share issues this same year. Since last May, executives have sold about $38 million in shares, according to a count by Open Insider.
</blockquote>
