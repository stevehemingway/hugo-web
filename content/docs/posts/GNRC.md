# Generac

We also took on a heavily promoted [Generac](https://sable.madmimi.com/c/21782?id=1429248.2676.1.fea64c5fa13e5962788ae0069c8acf01 "Click to open https://sable.madmimi.com/c/21782?id=1429248.2676.1.fea64c5fa13e5962788ae0069c8acf01") (NYSE: GNRC), which touts itself as a leader in home backup generators but has been pivoting to acquisitions in the clean energy space. We argued this pivot reflected challenges in its core business, and pointed to a strained channel partner where many U.S. customers alleged Generac had misrepresented the products’ benefits.([2](https://sable.madmimi.com/c/21782?id=1429248.2677.1.b1787f8b230d3deeb83c1a355fdadb0f "Click to open https://sable.madmimi.com/c/21782?id=1429248.2677.1.b1787f8b230d3deeb83c1a355fdadb0f")) The channel partner ultimately filed a lawsuit against Generac claiming defective products, and despite UBS claiming Generac was its top pick with more than 80% upside in June, we argued that 40–50% downside was more likely. Our estimates proved too conservative as the stock has declined by approximately -60%.
