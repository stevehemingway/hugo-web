# Raiffeisen Bank International AG ($RBI.DE)

## Status

System Exit: November 4, 2026 | Campaign Return: +10.6% | Investor Return: +2.4%

Grizzly alleged that Russian customs records link Raiffeisen's Russian unit to about US$1.19B of trade in sanction-restricted goods, that undercover contacts found staff open to evading sanctions, and that the bank understates €12.6B of cash trapped in Russia. Raiffeisen rejected the report, citing factual errors. The short ran for just under seven weeks from the €64.850 assumed entry on September 16, and the 8.2% drop into publication meant followers missed most of the downside, leaving the +2.4% Investor Return. The position peaked at 16.9% at our October 23 check, then eased to +10.6% at the exit (+12.3% market-adjusted, against a 1.7% gain in the STOXX 600).
