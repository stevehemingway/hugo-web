The ETRACS Monthly Pay 1.5x Leveraged Mortgage REIT ETN is a UBS-issued exchange-traded note listed on NYSE Arca, tracking the MVIS US Mortgage REITs Index at 1.5 times leverage, reset quarterly, with monthly coupon payments. The underlying index holds US mortgage REITs earning at least half their revenue from mortgage activity. Note the structure: this is an unsecured UBS obligation rather than a fund, so holders carry issuer credit risk on top of leveraged agency-prepayment exposure.
