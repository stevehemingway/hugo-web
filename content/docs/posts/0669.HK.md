# Techtronic Industries

Jehoshaphat Research [published](https://substack.com/redirect/0f30cbbb-ce7e-4fd6-b8dc-d48f84d2ce13?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/0f30cbbb-ce7e-4fd6-b8dc-d48f84d2ce13?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") on **Techtronic Industries** (Hong Kong: 0669 — HKD$144 billion), a power tool and outdoor equipment company. Jehoshaphat Research accused the company of widespread accounting fraud, primarily by “snowballing expenses into assets for over a decade [which results in] billions of dollars of assets [that] appear on the balance sheet that don’t really ‘exist’ except in an accounting sense.” Jehoshaphat Research alleged the company’s accounting fraud has resulted in [suspiciously stable and growing margins](https://substack.com/redirect/15c830ee-ab0d-4ce1-bbf9-2074d65dde15?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/15c830ee-ab0d-4ce1-bbf9-2074d65dde15?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") for a seasonal business and recent layoffs imply the “tide is going out” on the company.
