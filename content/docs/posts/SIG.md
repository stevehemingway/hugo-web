Signet Jewelers (NYSE: SIG — $2.56 billion) is the world’s largest retailer of diamond jewelry with around 2,7000 stores under brands including Zales, Kay, and Jared. Signet’s stores are often in malls and generally cater to the low-to-middle-income demographic, especially couples looking for engagement rings. The Bear Cave believes today’s youth and future generations will shift towards lab-grown diamond engagement rings and mined diamonds will be a vestige of the past.
