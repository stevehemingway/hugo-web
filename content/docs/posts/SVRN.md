# SVRN, Inc. (formerly OceanPal Inc.) — Fugazi Research Note Summary

**Source:** Substack newsletter email `SVRN_fugazi.eml` — Fugazi Research, 12 Oct 2026
**Title:** *SVRN: 100,000 to 1, the Ships Gone, the Audit Missing*
**Subject:** Short-seller-style takedown of SVRN, Inc., a Diana Shipping spinoff repurposed as a NEAR token treasury vehicle.

## Core thesis

SVRN isn't an AI infrastructure or digital-asset company — it's a small shipping company that was gutted and repurposed as a NEAR token treasury vehicle, while legacy shareholders were wiped out through repeated reverse splits. Fugazi calls the stock **uninvestable at any price above zero**.

## The key claims

**Dilution machine**
- July 2025: ~$18M raise via units + warrants with "zero-cash exercise" and share-count reset mechanics.
- Those warrants converted into **~149M new shares in 20 days**, for no cash (18.6M shares on 22 Jul → 167.4M on 11 Aug).
- **Two reverse splits in seven months** (25:1 then 20:1); a **500:1 factor this cycle**, **100,000:1 since 2022** (four splits total).
- A pre-offering holder of ~7.6M shares now holds ~15,200 shares — under 0.3% fully diluted.

**The "$120M PIPE" headfake**
- Headline: $120M. Actual cash: **~$13.7M**. The rest was ~$100M in NEAR tokens contributed by the NEAR Foundation itself.
- NEAR Foundation ended up beneficially owning **71.62%** on a fully exercised basis.
- ~$10.5M cash + $4.5M stock went to **old controllers** (Diana Shipping, Tuscany) to hand over control — i.e. new investors' cash paid the previous owners to leave.
- 102.96M shares registered for resale.

**Audit gone missing**
- **Ernst & Young resigned effective 28 Oct 2025** — the same day the transformation was announced.
- CBIZ engaged 26 Jan 2026, leaving a ~3-month gap with no auditor while the first digital-asset/staking/derivative transactions were booked.
- FY2025 **Form 20-F missed its deadline and its 12b-25 extension**. Nasdaq's outside date: **27 Oct 2026**.
- Audit committee (Veraros, Chrysochoidis) included two directors who were on the receiving side of the largest related-party transaction.

**Fleet sold to an insider**
- 31 Jul 2026: sold OP Vessel Holdco (last ships + JV stake) to **Sezali Inc.**, an affiliate of former Chairwoman Semiramis Paliou, for cancelled Series C prefs + $5M notes (~$17.2M) against ~$40.8M carrying value — **~$23.6M gap**, no fairness opinion, no independent valuation.

**Financials (preliminary, unaudited FY2025)**
- Revenue: $14.1M (down from ~$25.7M)
- Operating loss: $31.4M (from $18.0M)
- Net loss: $65.2M; loss attributable to common: **$70.0M** (~$4.96 lost per $1 of revenue)
- Year-end: $33.2M cash, $77.5M digital assets (~51.3M NEAR), equity $156.6M

**Treasury / NEAR concerns**
- Holdings bounce around (53.9M → 51.3M → ~55M → 55.5M) with no reconciliation; part is **pledged as collateral**.
- G-20 Group runs an unaudited "derivative overlay" on the position.
- Stated goal 10% of NEAR supply; at ~4% after 11 months, further accumulation needs new equity.
- NEAR ~76% below its Jan 2022 ATH ($20.40 → $4.88), with spot NEAR ETF filings a threat to the wrapper premium.

**Governance oddities**
- COO David Schwed got a larger inducement award than the CEO (1,096,205 vs 822,156 pre-split shares) — and is simultaneously listed as **CTO/organizer of OpenReserve Bank, N.A.** while SVRN's site still lists him as COO.
- Company website still shows pre-split stale data (53.9M NEAR, $1.23 price) and "Draft bio, pending approval" on two director pages.

## Bottom line

Fugazi's argument: common holders are paying full value for a wrapper controlled by insiders and the token issuer, with an unfinished audit, a 27 Oct 2026 delisting cliff, and a largest-holder resale path that opens the moment the audit lands. Ends with a standard "not investment advice; we may hold positions" disclaimer.

One note: this is a **single short-biased newsletter's opinion**, not an audited document — worth treating as an argument to verify against the underlying SEC filings rather than settled fact.
