Pelican Way Research published a short call on Shoals Technologies Group (NASDAQ: SHLS — solar balance-of-system components). Two claims form the case:

- **UGT Renewables / Sun Africa MOU** — Pelican Way says the counterparty to the announced memorandum of understanding is "in chaos due to the actions of its founder, a convicted felon", undermining the deal as support for the legacy business, which has suffered significant margin compression.
- **Data centre pivot** — the pivot depends heavily on battery company Terraflow, which has apparently raised only about $20m in total, and whose new headquarters was still under construction when Pelican Way's investigator visited earlier in October 2026.

Pelican Way directs readers to their website for the full thesis and for their disclaimer on how they hold and profit from their financial interests.
