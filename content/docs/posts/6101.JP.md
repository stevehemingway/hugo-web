Tsugami Corporation (ツガミ株式会社) is a Japanese precision machine tool manufacturer founded in 1937 and headquartered in Tokyo. The company specializes in CNC (computer numerical control) machine tools used primarily in the automotive, medical device, aerospace, and electronics industries.

**Business Segments**

Tsugami operates through five geographic segments: Japan, China, India, Korea, and Other Regions. The company's product portfolio includes:

- **CNC Precision Automatic Lathes**: Core product line featuring gang-tool post configurations, turret systems, and vertical designs for high-precision small part production
- **Turning Centers**: Bar and chuck work machines for larger component machining
- **Machining Centers**: Vertical and horizontal configurations for complex milling operations
- **Precision Grinding Machines**: CNC cylindrical grinders, small grinders, and carbide grinding equipment
- **Precision Thread and Form Rolling Machines**: For specialized threading applications

China represents a significant market for Tsugami, with Precision Tsugami (China) Corporation ranking as the top foreign-owned CNC high-precision machine tool manufacturer in China by revenue.
