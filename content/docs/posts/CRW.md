Craneware plc is a Scottish healthcare software company listed on AIM. It sells revenue-integrity and pricing-analytics tools to US hospitals and health systems, chiefly its Trisus platform, which charges hospitals correctly for the services they deliver so claims are not under-recovered. Customers are contracted on multi-year subscriptions, so revenue is recurring and visible, and the shareholder story has historically rested on that predictability rather than on volume growth.
