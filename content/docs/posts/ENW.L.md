# Enwell Energy

Enwell Energy plc is a United Kingdom-based gas, condensate and liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) production company. The Company’s business activity is oil and gas exploration, development and production. Its primary operations are located in Ukraine. The Company operates four gas and condensate fields in the Dnipro-Donets basin in north-east Ukraine: Mekhedivsko-Golotvshchinske (MEX-GOL) and Svyrydivske (SV), Vasyshchivske (VAS) and Svystunkivsko-Chervonolutske (SC). It holds a 100% working interest in and are the operator of the MEX-GOL and SV fields. It holds two licenses in Ukraine’s Poltava region, which adjacent and extend over a combined area of approximately 269 square kilometers (km2), approximately 200 kilometers (km) east of Kyiv and 170 km north-west of Poltava. It holds license in VAS field, which extends over an area of approximately 33.2 km2 and is located 17 km south-east of Kharkiv, in the Kharkiv region of Ukraine.
