# Egdon Resources

Egdon Resources PLC is a United Kingdom-based independent onshore focused oil and gas exploration and production company. The Company holds interests in approximately 37 licenses in the United Kingdom and has an active program of exploration, appraisal and development within its portfolio of oil and gas assets. The Company is an approved operator in the United Kingdom. The Company’s projects include Gainsborough Trough, Wressle, Biscathorpe, and North Kelsey located in East Midlands; Resolution and Endeavour located in North East England/North Sea; Weald Basin, and Wessex Basin located in Southern England. The Company also acquires several license interests in the North West, including PEDL141, PEDL191, EXL253 and PEDL039. The Company’s shale-gas acreage position in Northern England is approximately 164,280 net acres. It holds interests in approximately 71,361 net acres in Gainsborough Trough.
