I. Overview

More than a month ago, I posted an article here on Medium to disclose the fraudulent accounting activities committed by Western Alliance Bancorporation (WAL), with the title of “Strategy of Fraudulent Accounting and Data Fabrication — — Isn’t It a Smart Move to Fight against Stressed Situation?” (accessible through the link https://medium.com/@zatoichigo/strategy-of-fraudulent-accounting-and-data-fabrication-isnt-it-a-smart-move-to-fight-against-34e8e5cf744d).
