# Vertiv Holdings

Independent short-seller Keith Dalrymple [published](https://twitter.com/Jonathan_Keith2/status/1600159684152872961) a second article on **Vertiv Holdings** (NYSE: VRT — $5.15 billion), a data center equipment and services company. The article claims Vertiv dramatically overpaid for its acquisition of E&I Engineering last year, fitting a pattern of enthusiastic forecasts that “end in disappointment.” [Last month](https://thebearcave.substack.com/p/the-bear-cave-144), Dalrymple also alleged the company was booking billions in unprofitable business “to project the optics of growth.”
