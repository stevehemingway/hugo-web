# F&G Annuities & Life, Inc. ($FG)

## Bear case

Mispriced Assets published a short report on F&G Annuities & Life (NYSE: FG — US$3.05 billion), arguing that investment losses or increased withdrawals could force a capital raise that wipes out common shareholders. The report questions the insurer's exposure to private credit and its reliance on regulatory accounting exceptions.

F&G's quarterly filing says two reinsurance subsidiaries would fall below minimum capital requirements without those permissions.

Author Nick Nemeth also released a companion website with interactive graphics, stress tests, and a chatbot for querying the research.
