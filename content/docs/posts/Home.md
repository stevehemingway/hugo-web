This is a sort of scrapbook, where I make notes on stocks, and trends, 
that catch my eye.

I am trying to use tags to keep it organized.
The general idea is to identify potential trades (#potential),
and whether they are long (#potential/long) or short (you get the idea).

I often post links to seekingalpha or similar sites, but also note the author
in the tags (e.g. #analyst/blue-orca-research). 

I also try to identify *why* a stock is a buy (or a sell), e.g. #thesis/fraud.

I keep the files organized locally using obsidian, which works well, 
but everything is just markdown, so you can git clone and just edit.
It would be wonderful if you turned this into a collaborative venture.
Just ask to be added. Or you could fork this, I guess. I am a bit of a git noob, 
tbh, so maybe that'd be better, but it'd be nice to combine forces.

Thanks for reading!

Steve.
