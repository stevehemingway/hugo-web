## _**A+ Free Resources**_

1.  [SEC Full-Text Search](https://substack.com/redirect/47482651-fb66-44bc-974a-7a01ddcb803c?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/47482651-fb66-44bc-974a-7a01ddcb803c?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") — Search through 20 years of SEC filings for specific terms, people, or entities. The Bear Cave commonly uses this tool to see every instance where a person’s name was mentioned in a SEC filing
    
2.  [PCAOB Auditor Search](https://substack.com/redirect/eaa1405b-ac48-4a9e-8f01-8893379b5703?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/eaa1405b-ac48-4a9e-8f01-8893379b5703?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") — Find the auditor and specific audit partner for any company, as well as their track record. Use the search bar in the top right and click auditor search
    
3.  [SocialBlade](https://substack.com/redirect/7d825ce2-c672-41bf-95d5-521a2f6ef87c?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/7d825ce2-c672-41bf-95d5-521a2f6ef87c?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") — Track social media growth of a company or individual
    
4. 5.  [OpenCorporates](https://substack.com/redirect/63f03d53-d008-466c-863f-23355f33317a?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/63f03d53-d008-466c-863f-23355f33317a?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") — Quickly find the executives, board members, or state registration for private businesses
    
6.  [Wayback Machine](https://substack.com/redirect/2c601adb-f970-4ac4-844f-ee4775c226a3?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98 "Click to open https://substack.com/redirect/2c601adb-f970-4ac4-844f-ee4775c226a3?j=eyJ1Ijoibm1idCJ9.oKN8sNuAta-fKDvVXVf40Ijrk4j2KaDlakvdPE7BH98") — A non-profit that regularly archives millions of the most visited sites.
