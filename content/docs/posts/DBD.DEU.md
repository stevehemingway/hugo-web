Diebold Nixdorf makes ATMs, self-checkouts and point-of-sale systems for banks and retailers. The two halves came together in the 2016 merger of Diebold and Wincor Nixdorf, a deal weighted with debt that drove the combined company through a 2023 Chapter 11 and out to its lenders, who now own most of the equity. The installed base and service contracts are genuinely sticky, but hardware margins are thin and the business carries substantial pension and lease obligations.
