Tags

Every post is tagged across eight roots: analyst, sector, thesis, market, potential, focus, size and theme. Tags nest with /, so market/london sits under market.

Under focus, a post is either focus/security (a traceable listed issuer), focus/theme (a commodity or sector thesis with no single issuer), focus/method (a technique or screen rather than a company), or focus/non-issuer (a stub, image or otherwise unidentifiable subject). Only focus/security posts can carry prices.
